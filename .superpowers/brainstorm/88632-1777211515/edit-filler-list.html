A

Two sections, distinct affordances

Default Library: tap capsule to toggle on/off (no ✕). Your Words: each has ✕ for delete, plus add input. Clear separation; no risk of accidentally "deleting" a default.

B

Unified pool with "default" badge

One section. Every capsule has ✕. ✕ on default → toggles off (still visible, dimmed). ✕ on custom → permanent delete. Compact; one mental model. Risk: ✕ on default that was already off needs different feedback (re-enable?).
