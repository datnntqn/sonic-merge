Why Export isn't on Smart Cut / Denoise homes:

Those screens are recents lists. There's no single concrete file to export from a list — you'd have to pick one first. Export becomes meaningful only after you tap a session and run Apply Cuts (Smart Cut) or finish denoising (Denoise) — that's when an output file actually exists.

Why Merge home has Export:

Merge home is the editor. There's no list, no push navigation, the timeline you build IS the workspace. So Export earns its toolbar slot there in a way it doesn't on Smart Cut/Denoise homes.

Pattern reference: Voice Memos puts Share on the playback screen (after you tap a recording), not on the recordings list. Photos puts Export on a selected photo. Same logic.
